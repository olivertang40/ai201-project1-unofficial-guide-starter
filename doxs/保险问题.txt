保险问题

1. 学校提供的医疗保险怎么样？

答：全年保险费约为1300美元，PPO

2. 可以使用自己的医疗保险吗？

答：All international students on an F-1visa must have health insurance through Trine University. Waivers will ONLY be provided for sponsored students(government sponsor, employer). Individual and private insurance plan purchased by student DOES NOT qualify for a waiver.

3. 医疗保险包含牙齿和眼科保险吗？

答：不包含，但可以自选添加，需额外付费。

4. 与学校合作的保险公司是哪家？

答：United Health Care
